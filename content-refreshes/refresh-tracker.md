# Content Refresh Tracker

Tracks when existing blog posts and pages were last reviewed and updated. Refreshing content (updating facts, adding new sections, improving internal links) helps maintain rankings over time.

## When to refresh a post

- Rankings or traffic have dropped.
- Information is out of date (pricing, technology, guidelines).
- A competitor has published more thorough content.
- The post is 12+ months old and has not been reviewed.

## Refresh checklist

- [ ] Verify all facts, stats, and any references are current.
- [ ] Update the title and meta description if they can be improved.
- [ ] Add or update internal links to current service pages.
- [ ] Check that all links resolve (no 404s).
- [ ] Improve readability (headings, short paragraphs, lists).
- [ ] Confirm images have descriptive alt text.
- [ ] Update the "last modified" date in the schema and frontmatter.
