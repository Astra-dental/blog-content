# Blog Internal Linking Plan

Guidelines for linking blog posts to service pages and to each other on astradentalcentre.com. Internal links help users navigate and pass SEO value to key money pages.

## Principles

- Every blog post should link to at least one relevant service page and to a conversion page (book appointment or contact).
- Use descriptive anchor text that includes the target keyword, not "click here".
- Link to related blog posts to keep readers on site and build topic clusters.
- Do not over-link: aim for 2-5 contextual internal links per post.

## Notes

- Verify each destination URL exists and resolves (no 404s) before publishing.
- Update this map whenever new service pages or posts are added.
- Anchor text should read naturally within the sentence, never forced.
