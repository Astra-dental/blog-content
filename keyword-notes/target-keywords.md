Working list of target keywords for Astra Dental Centre blog and service content, organized by topic. Search volumes and difficulty should be confirmed with a keyword tool (e.g. Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs) before prioritizing.

- are dental implants worth it
- how to whiten teeth safely
- childs first dental visit

## Branded / trust keywords

- astra dental centre
- dr potluri dentist langley

## Notes on intent

- "langley" + service terms = high commercial intent; point these at service pages, not blog posts.
- "vs", "how to", "cost", "what to do" = informational intent; target with blog posts that link to service pages.
- Avoid keyword stuffing. Use one primary keyword per page plus natural variations.
