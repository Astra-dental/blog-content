## How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Most Invisalign cases take 12 to 18 months, though mild corrections can finish sooner and complex cases may take longer. We give you a personalized estimate at your consultation.

## Is Invisalign more expensive than braces?

Invisalign and braces often cost a similar amount, depending on the complexity of your case. We review pricing and any insurance or CDCP coverage during your visit so there are no surprises.

## Does Invisalign hurt?

Most patients feel mild pressure for a day or two when they switch to a new aligner. This is a normal sign your teeth are moving and usually fades quickly.

## How often do I need to wear the aligners?

For the best results, wear your aligners 20 to 22 hours a day, removing them only to eat, drink anything other than water, and brush your teeth.

## Can I eat normally with Invisalign?

Yes. Because the aligners are removable, there are no food restrictions. Just take them out before eating and brush before putting them back in.
